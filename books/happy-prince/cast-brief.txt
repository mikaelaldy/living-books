CUT-PAPER CHARACTER RULES, which override everything else:
- No outlines. Never stroke a body, limb or face edge. Every shape is a flat paper fill, with one darker and one lighter paper shape on top for shading, plus small details.
- Limbs are thick tapered filled paths with rounded overlapping ends, so a joint never looks like a doll's hinge. Never draw circles or dots at joints.
- People are not chibi: the head is about a quarter of the height, with real hands, expressive eyes and clothing with folds drawn as shading shapes. Adults stand 200 to 230 px, children 140 to 170 px.
- The Happy Prince is a tall gilded statue on a column: he stands 220 px on the column top, with a stiff dignified body, a long coat, a sword with a large red ruby in its hilt, and two bright sapphire eyes. His gold is layered: #D9B24C base, #F2D580 highlights, #A9812C shadows. Statues never move their feet: only the head, arms, and tears move. State is drawn with f.state: 0 gilded with ruby and both eyes, 1 ruby gone (an empty socket), 2 one sapphire gone, 3 both eyes gone (dull socket holes), 4 stripped to dull grey-brown lead with a plain heart. Tears are drawn when f.tears is true as bright drops running down the cheeks.
- The Swallow is a small, neat, quick bird, about 70 px across, with a forked tail, a rust-coloured throat and dark blue-black wings. Jointed wings (shoulder, wing tip) and a tail. f.carry shows a jewel in its beak: 'ruby', 'sapphire' or none. Its dead pose lies on its back with its wings open and its feet up.
- The Reed is a tall slender river reed with a leafy head, swaying through its poses: bow, curtsey, sway1 and sway2, and it can be drawn in water ripples.
- The Seamstress is a thin worn woman in a plain dress; the sick boy lies in a small bed; the student is a young man with brown curls and a coat; the little match-girl is bare-headed and barefoot in a ragged dress with a match bundle; the Mayor is a portly man in a robe and chain; the townsfolk rig has f.variant 0 to 3 changing clothes (Charity Children in scarlet cloaks and white pinafores for variant 3); the Angel is a luminous winged figure.
=====
For each character, read the pages and design EVERY pose the book needs, plus walk poses where the character walks. The Prince's poses: stand, gaze (head turned), weep (head bowed), give (arm out, hand open), and stand again for later states. The Swallow: perch, fly1, fly2, sleep (head tucked), peck, shiver, droop, dead. The Reed: bow, curtsey, sway1, sway2. The Seamstress: sew (seated at a table), prick (finger raised), sleep (head on the table). The boy: lie, toss (turning in bed), sit (sitting up). The student: write (at a desk), despair (hands to head), joy (holding a jewel up). The match-girl: stand, cry, hold, shiver. The Mayor: stand, strut, point, gaze. Townsfolk: stand, walk1, walk2, talk, shiver, play. The Angel: fly1, fly2, carry (arms holding a small leaden heart and a dead bird, drawn when f.carry is true).
Sitting, lying in bed, crying and writing are all distinct body shapes, not small changes. Props are bones drawn only when f.prop names them, such as a table with thimble, a desk, matches, a basket.
