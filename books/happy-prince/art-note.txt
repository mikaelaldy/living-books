BOOK-SPECIFIC LIGHT, which overrides the SKY AND LIGHT rule above: most of this story happens at night or in winter. Night skies are deep plum and indigo with a big pale moon, stars as small paper cut-outs, and warm golden light from windows, lamps and the gilded statue itself, never pitch black and never grey. Day skies are warm cream and peach. Winter scenes use pale silver-blue snow and ice with warm lit windows. The column: the Happy Prince stands on top of a tall stone column; make the ground line the top of the column under his feet, with the city rooftops far below and distant. Keep the characters well lit against the background.
